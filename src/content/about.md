## My story

I fell in love with computers at an arcade machine in a little wooden hut by the beach in Banate, Iloilo, in the Philippines, right next to a corner shop. It was Mortal Kombat. I used to sneak out as a kid and run there to play it. By seven I was the one my older cousin asked how to work the family computer.

I always loved drawing, and my family knew me for it. As I got older I moved it onto the computer. I made Minecraft texture packs in Photoshop, and my own skins and capes, pixel by pixel, and shared them with friends. I used Photopea for fun. I opened YouTube tutorials to learn Cinema 4D, and I made logos and intros and enjoyed the animating side. I even posted a tutorial on how to download fonts from DaFont.

I also made private servers for browser games. That meant port forwarding, MySQL, phpMyAdmin and Hamachi, and the wifi would drop out while I was testing so my friends could get on. It was all fun to me. I made websites on Wix just out of curiosity, and I became the person friends and family asked for computer help and edits.

As a teenager I got good at competitive games. I was one of the UK's highest-ranked Apex Legends players for several seasons. I travelled to the Apex Legends Open LAN in New Orleans to play with two online friends I'd never met in person. It was one of the best experiences of my life.

People who had no time started asking me to coach them and help them rank up, so I opened an eBay shop, and it still has 100% positive feedback. Then I started the Instagram page for it. I made every graphic and video myself. I woke up to customer enquiries and answered them professionally with one eye open. That's when I realised I love marketing and seeing things grow.

I spent a couple of years at university, then moved into a marketing apprenticeship at BlayCare in March 2025. I'm now the Marketing Assistant, I rebuilt the website, and I passed my Level 3 Multichannel Marketer qualification in October 2026.

Now I'm building small tools to help me work better, and I want to keep growing. I'm still finding my path, but I have a much better idea of it than I used to.

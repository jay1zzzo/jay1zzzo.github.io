## My story

By seven I was the one my older cousin asked to work the family computer. I'd found a Mortal Kombat arcade machine in a little wooden hut near a corner shop, by the beach opposite my home in Banate, Iloilo, in the Philippines. I used to sneak out as a kid and run there to play it.

I always loved drawing, and my family knew me for it. The first drawing I remember is Simba from The Lion King. I made kites, and I wondered about space, rockets and aeroplanes.

As I got older I moved drawing onto the computer. I made Minecraft texture packs in Photoshop, and my own skins and capes, pixel by pixel, and shared them with friends. Then I started watching FaZe montages and edits on YouTube and wanted to know how people made them. That led me to Sony Vegas, After Effects and Cinema 4D, from YouTube tutorials and a lot of trial and error. I made banners, logos, intros and gaming montages for myself and my friends. I didn't think of it as learning design or editing. I just liked making things and working out how they worked.

The same curiosity took me into the technical side. I built and upgraded my own PC. I made private servers for browser games, which meant port forwarding, MySQL, phpMyAdmin and Hamachi, and the wifi would drop out while I was testing so my friends could get on. I made websites on Wix out of curiosity, and I became the person friends and family asked for computer help and edits.

As a teenager I got good at competitive games. I was one of the UK's highest-ranked Apex Legends players for several seasons. I was flown out to the Apex Legends Open LAN in New Orleans to play with two online friends I'd never met in person. It was one of the best experiences of my life.

Then people who had no time started asking me to coach them and help them rank up, so I started selling that help online. I opened an eBay shop, which still has 100% positive feedback, and an Instagram page offering the same services. I also answered customer enquiries professionally, including the early-morning ones, with one eye open. That's when I realised I love marketing and seeing things grow.

I spent a couple of years at university, doing a foundation year and then starting an architecture degree, before moving into a marketing apprenticeship at BlayCare in March 2025. I'm now the Marketing Assistant. I rebuilt the website, wrote the brand guidelines and have worked on social media and newsletters. I passed my Level 3 Multichannel Marketer qualification in October 2026.

Alongside the training from my apprenticeship with Althaus, a lot of my practical learning has been self-directed. As the sole in-house marketer, I've taken initiative, picked up new tools and worked through problems on my own.

More recently I've become interested in AI and what it makes possible. Not just generating content, but building things, solving problems and making everyday tasks easier. I've built a productivity app, an MCP integration and some other small tools. I built them with AI's help, and I'm still learning.

I'm still the person colleagues call when a computer won't behave. I'm a junior who wants to join a team, contribute now and learn from people with more experience.

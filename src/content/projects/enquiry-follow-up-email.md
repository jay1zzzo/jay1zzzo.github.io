## The brief

Families often enquire about care, then go quiet while they think, compare providers or talk it over. This email follows up the day after an enquiry, so they know what happens next and have one clear reason to take the next step.

This is a concept I made to practise email marketing. It uses BlayCare's real brand, banner, CQC rating and accreditations, but it has not been sent.

## Who it's for

- **A family member** who has just enquired about care for someone close to them, or for themselves.
- This is one of the two audiences I defined for the BlayCare website. The other is professional referrers, such as social workers, who would get a different email.

## Where it sits in the journey

1. Someone fills in the enquiry form on the BlayCare website.
2. **This email** arrives the next day.
3. The team phones them to talk through the support needed.
4. A team leader visits for a free care assessment at home.

## What's in the email, and why

- **Subject line:** "Thank you for getting in touch with BlayCare", so they recognise it straight away.
- **Preview text:** "What happens next, and who you'll speak to", the line shown in the inbox before it's opened.
- **One photo at the top**, to make it feel warm and human before any text.
- **One clear heading:** "Thank you for getting in touch. Here's what happens next."
- **The two real next steps**, a phone call and a free care assessment, numbered so they can be skimmed.
- **One call to action:** "Book your free care assessment", with the phone number as a backup for people who would rather talk.
- **Trust signals near the end**, not the top: the CQC rating, the BlayCare banner and the accreditations.
- **The legal extras:** an unsubscribe link, a privacy notice link and the company details.

## Decisions

- **Brand guidelines throughout.** Primary Blue for the button and step numbers, Secondary Blue for subheadings, Soft Blue for the panel, Charcoal for text, Poppins for headings and Inter for body text.
- **A single 600px column.** It reads the same on a phone, where most emails are opened.
- **No hard sell.** The wording is calm and reassuring, because the reader may be worried about someone they love.
- **CQC's own rating display.** I rebuilt it from CQC's official template, not a home-made badge.

## What I would track

These are what I would measure if it were sent. There are no results, because it has not been.

- How many people open it.
- How many click "Book your free care assessment".
- How many assessments are booked after the email.
- Replies and unsubscribes, to check the tone is right.

## What I would do next

- Build it in Wix's email marketing and set it up as an automation triggered by the enquiry form.
- Write a second version for professional referrers.

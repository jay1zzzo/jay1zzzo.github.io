## Overview

A community coffee morning at the BlayCare office in aid of Macmillan Cancer Support, for staff, carers, clients and families. I supported the planning and made the promotional materials. Afterwards I published a short highlight video.

## Purpose and audience

- Encourage people to come and to donate.
- Show BlayCare's care and community values.
- Create content to help local visibility and engagement.

It was not a lead-generation exercise.

## My contribution

Colleagues helped with the setup and on the day. My work included:

- Invitation design and wording, and the tear-off RSVP slip.
- Printed invitations and personal letters to clients.
- Reminders in the September newsletter, and social posts and captions.
- Staff coordination.
- Helping prepare the room, tables, music, refreshments, donations and wheelchair access, with colleagues.
- Publishing content after the event, including a short highlight video made in CapCut.

**Design credit:** I adapted a Macmillan Canva template into BlayCare branding, and I also created layouts using existing brand assets. The Macmillan elements are not my original artwork.

## Decisions

- **Printed invitations, two to an A4 sheet.** This made them simpler to print and hand out. Printed invitations suited clients, and carers could deliver them and collect replies.
- **A paper reply slip.** It gave people an alternative to booking online, and replies helped plan attendance and refreshments.
- **Different channels for different people.** This is my own judgement, not something I measured. Personal letters suited clients, the newsletter and staff messages worked for internal reminders, and social posts after the event were better for visibility.
- **Monday.com for planning.** I used it to track deadlines, tasks, progress and priorities. I also made a shopping checklist. The planning budget was about £100. That is an estimate, not audited spend.
- **Photo consent.** I thought about consent before any identifiable photos were used for workplace marketing. Photos from the day are not shown here, because workplace consent does not automatically cover a personal portfolio.
- **Sharing the highlight video.** I posted it on Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn. Macmillan interacted with the online content.

## Outcomes

- About £50 was donated. I do not treat this as a return on the budget.
- Views were 954 from 4 September to 1 October, compared with 363 in the previous 28 days.
- Separately, views were up 550 percent (403 views) from 25 September to 1 October, compared with the previous 7 days. The percentage changes with the exact seven days chosen, so I treat it as a snapshot.

These are account-level view totals. Not all of them necessarily came from the event, so I do not claim the event caused them.

The RSVP records are no longer available, so I have not included attendance or response numbers.

## What I would do differently

These are things I would change, not results.

- Start promotion earlier.
- Track RSVPs and actual attendance more consistently.
- Gather structured feedback from attendees.
- Keep the results together after the event.

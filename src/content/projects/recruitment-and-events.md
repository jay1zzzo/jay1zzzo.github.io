## Care quiz

Four question pages from a thirty-question multiple-choice care quiz, in BlayCare branding. There is also a separate staff answer key, which is not shown here. A note printed on the answer key suggests using the quiz as an icebreaker at a recruitment fair.

This is a supporting example only. I have not confirmed who wrote the questions, how the quiz was used or what came of it, so none of that is claimed. It is not presented as training or as an assessment.

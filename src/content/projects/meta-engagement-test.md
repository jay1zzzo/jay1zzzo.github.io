## Overview

A small paid test on Facebook. I promoted an existing BlayCare service post to see if a little spend could help more people in Derby see it and engage with it.

## Purpose and audience

I wanted to know if a small paid promotion could increase visibility and engagement among people relevant to private home care in Derby.

I chose Facebook because families researching care use it for information and trust signals.

## My contribution

I set up the promotion, watched it while it ran, corrected it and reported on it. This was part of my apprenticeship work at BlayCare.

## Decisions

- **An existing post.** I promoted a branded BlayCare service post instead of making new advert creative. It let me run a quick pilot.
- **Engagement as the objective.** I did not choose leads, purchases or website conversions.
- **A small budget.** I first set £12 (£4 a day for 3 days). Before launch I reduced it to £9 planned (£3 a day for 3 days).
- **Stopping early.** I stopped it after about two days. Actual spend was £6.30.
- **Fixing the audience while live.** I started with a broad adult audience, then corrected the location to Derby and Derbyshire. While it was running, I saw the location had expanded to the whole United Kingdom. I think Meta's Advantage+ setting was involved, but I have not verified that. I narrowed it back to Derby and Derbyshire and changed the caption at the same time.

## Results

| Measure | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Actual spend | £6.30 |
| Post engagements | 40 |
| Cost per engagement | About £0.16 (£6.30 divided by 40, rounded) |
| Views | 443 |
| Reach | 380 people |
| Visible likes on the post | From 1 to over 40 |
| Desktop impressions | 4 of 439 |

The like count is something I observed. It is not controlled proof that the promotion caused it. Meta reports views and impressions as separate measures, so those two numbers do not match.

Almost all delivery was on mobile. That is useful to know for design.

## Outcomes

This was a test of visibility and engagement only. No leads, enquiries, bookings or revenue were reported or proved. Engagement is not sales, and I am not treating it as a return on investment.

Because I changed the location and the caption at the same time, I cannot tell which change made a difference.

## What I would do differently

- Use a lead objective or form with a clear call to action if enquiries are the goal.
- Use tighter local audience controls.
- Record a baseline before starting.
- Change one thing at a time, so I can read the results properly.

## What I learned

- Keeping to a budget.
- Spotting and fixing a targeting problem while a promotion is live.
- The honest limits of a very small test.
- Using the data to choose the next experiment.

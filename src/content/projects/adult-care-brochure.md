## Overview

A folded brochure that introduces BlayCare and what it offers. I prepared a print version and a digital version.

## Purpose and audience

The readers were families choosing care and professionals making referrals.

- Introduce BlayCare and its services.
- Help families understand their care options.
- Support professional referrals.
- Give families a straightforward route to an enquiry form. The printed version has a QR code for this. It is removed in the copies shown here.

## My contribution

- Planned the structure and the order of the panels.
- Wrote or edited the service descriptions.
- Designed the layout.
- Selected images and brand assets.
- Prepared the print and digital versions.
- Checked the details with management.
- Arranged lamination through Ryman.

## Decisions

- **Plain language.** Services are described simply, in short sections.
- **Organised around the reader.** The information follows what families and referrers need to know.
- **Clear next steps.** Contact details and the route to an enquiry are easy to find.
- **Two formats.** The files were adapted for print and for digital use.

## Outcomes

Printed copies were distributed. I do not have enquiry or conversion figures for the brochure, so none are claimed.

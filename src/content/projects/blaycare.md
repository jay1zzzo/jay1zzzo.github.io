## The problem

BlayCare needed stronger online visibility, clearer branding, better website usability and clearer communication.

## What I did

- Rebuilt the website in Wix Studio, with new copy, service pages, careers pages and enquiry forms. It launched on 26 June 2026.
- Used competitor benchmarking to find weaknesses in structure, SEO and trust signals.
- Wrote separate personas for professional referrers and private families.

Tools: Canva, Wix Studio, CapCut, Buffer, SEOptimer.

## What happened

- The SEOptimer grade is now A+.
- The careers page had 7 recruitment enquiries in its first two months.

## Overview

I rebuilt BlayCare's website in Wix Studio. It has new copy, service pages, careers pages and enquiry forms, and it launched on 26 June 2026.

## Purpose and audience

BlayCare needed stronger online visibility, clearer branding, better website usability and communication.

I wrote separate personas for the two main audiences: professional referrers and private families. They came from desk research and what I learned inside the business, not from interviews.

- **Local authority referrers** care about speed, suitability and professional communication.
- **Private families** care about reassurance, dignity, visible reviews and information they can understand.

## Research before building

Before I built anything, I looked at what a family sees when they search locally. I searched Google for "home care Derby", "domiciliary care Derby" and "private home care Derby", and compared three local competitors.

I also used the SEOptimer tool to benchmark their sites. I reviewed their structure, usability, service information, trust signals and calls to action. The aim was to find gaps BlayCare could improve, not to copy anyone.

## My contribution

- Rebuilt the website in Wix Studio.
- Wrote the new copy.
- Built the service pages, careers pages and enquiry forms.
- Benchmarked local competitors before building.
- Used the Wix Studio editor and CMS to organise images and update text, visuals, layout and calls to action.
- Worked on brand guidelines to give the site a clearer, more consistent look. These are on a separate page.

## Decisions

- **Two audiences.** Separate personas for professional referrers and private families shaped what each page needed to say.
- **Benchmarking first.** The research showed where the old site was weak.
- **Choosing the platform.** I compared WordPress.com, Wix, Wix Studio and v0 for design flexibility, ease of maintenance by the team, responsiveness and fit for the business. I recommended Wix Studio. I only used v0 to explore ideas and mock-ups, not for the live site.
- **A clear next step.** Enquiry forms and careers pages give each visitor somewhere to go.

## What I recommended

From the research, I recommended earlier trust signals, clearer service copy, visible CQC regulator information, reviews where they are properly sourced, and simpler ways to enquire. These are recommendations. I am not claiming all of them are on the live site.

## Outcomes

The SEOptimer audit grade is now A+. This is an audit score for the site's technical and on-page checks. It is not a measure of enquiries or sales.

I have not included traffic or enquiry figures, because I do not have dated sources for them to show here.

## What I learned

To justify creative decisions with research, audience needs and performance data.

I also taught myself Wix Studio techniques and landing page structure from YouTube tutorials, and applied them to this work.

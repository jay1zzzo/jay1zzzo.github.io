## Overview

BlayCare's brand guidelines cover logos, colours, typography and tone of voice.

## Purpose and audience

BlayCare's branding looked inconsistent, there were no written rules, different people made materials in their own way, and the old look was outdated. The guidelines are for anyone who makes BlayCare materials.

## My contribution

I set the colour palette, the fonts and the logo rules. I decided the tone of voice with my manager.

## Where it is applied

- Website
- Social media
- Newsletters

The examples below are BlayCare materials in the same colours and fonts.

## What I learned

Writing the rules down helps other people follow them. Templates save time. Consistency builds trust.

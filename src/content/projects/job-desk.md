## The problem

My job applications were scattered, it was hard to track what I'd applied for, and it was hard to judge which jobs fit me.

## What I did

I adapted an existing open-source app into a job search workspace for myself. It's AI-assisted: AI tools did much of the coding. I set the requirements, coordinated and reviewed the work.

- Five tabs: Jobs, Writer, Applications, Stand out and Your profile.
- It collects vacancies, reviews how well they suit me, helps prepare applications and tracks progress.
- Nine layouts, six appearance modes and nine accent colours, plus a custom colour.
- It runs locally in a browser on my Mac. It's not a public website.

## What I learned

Personal data should stay local, so private details stay on my own machine. Building in small steps and testing each one works. I learned that I can fix real problems and make them easier with the help of AI.

## What I did

I adapted an existing open-source app into a job search workspace for myself. It's AI-assisted: AI tools did much of the coding. I set the requirements, coordinated and reviewed the work, and tested it on Windows.

- Five tabs: Jobs, Writer, Applications, Stand out and Your profile.
- It collects vacancies, reviews how well they suit me, helps prepare applications and tracks progress.
- Nine layouts, six appearance modes and nine accent colours, plus a custom colour.
- It runs locally in a browser, with Mac and Windows launchers. It's not a public website.
- My data syncs between my computers through a private Git branch. Saves are kept on the computer while offline, and conflicting changes are kept for me to review.
- 31 tests passed on Windows.

## Overview

A graphic for International Women's Day, published on the company's social media.

## Purpose and audience

- Recognise the women working at BlayCare.
- Celebrate the team publicly.
- Show the people behind the organisation.
- Take part in International Women's Day.

## My contribution

- Took the photographs and selected the staff photographs.
- Removed backgrounds and edited the images.
- Designed the layout and graphics.
- Wrote the headline and caption.
- Published or scheduled the post.

**Design credit:** I adapted an existing template. The design is not wholly original.

## Decisions

- **Real photographs of the team.** They put the people at the centre of the post, which fitted the aim of showing who is behind the organisation.
- **A short headline and caption.** They kept the message simple.

## Outcomes

The post was published on the company's social media. I have not included reach or engagement figures here.

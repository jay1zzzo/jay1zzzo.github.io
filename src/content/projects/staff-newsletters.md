## Overview

BlayCare Monthly is a newsletter for staff. Two pages are shown: a first page with the regular sections and a wellbeing page. Staff names and photographs are blurred, and other pages are not shown here.

## Purpose and audience

The audience was BlayCare staff. Editions went out through WhatsApp or another staff messaging app, so this was not an email campaign.

- Company updates.
- Staff recognition.
- Wellbeing advice and resources.

## My contribution

- Designed the layouts.
- Planned the recurring sections and gathered the updates.
- Wrote and edited the copy.
- Selected images and graphics.
- Added links and interactive elements.
- Prepared and distributed the finished editions.

A manager reviewed each edition before it was distributed.

## Decisions

- **Easy to scan.** Clear headings and short sections, with images and coloured panels to organise the information.
- **Familiar.** Consistent brand colours and fonts, and recurring sections so staff knew where to look.
- **Easy next steps.** Clickable links, and changes informed by feedback on earlier editions.

## Outcomes

Staff responded to prompts such as Google Form reminders for staff profiles. I do not have readership or click-through figures, so none are claimed.

## Overview

Two pieces of festive communication: an invitation to a Christmas Winter Party, and greeting cards for Christmas 2025. I made the invitation in a print size and a newsletter size.

## Purpose and audience

- **Party invitation:** staff, clients and families.
- **Greeting cards:** staff, clients, families and business contacts.

## My contribution

- Created the layouts using existing brand assets.
- Wrote or edited the wording.
- Selected and adjusted colours, fonts and images.
- Prepared different sizes and print files.
- Placed the invitation in a newsletter.
- Emailed the materials to business contacts and friends of the company.

## Decisions

- **One message, several formats.** The invitation exists as a print file and a newsletter version, so it could be shared through staff messaging, email and the newsletter.
- **Brand assets throughout.** The cards and invitation reuse BlayCare's brand assets so they look like they come from the same organisation.

## Outcomes

The materials were shared through staff messaging or email, and the invitation was included in a published newsletter. The finished designs are available. I did not manage RSVPs for the event, so no response or attendance figures are given.
